The Real Story

The complete, true story of Brothers: A Tale of Two Sons (Starbreeze Studios, 2013; Remake 2024) — the same story this website's quiz is built on.

⚠️ SPOILER WARNING: This page tells the full real story, including the ending. The quiz questions come from these events — so reading the story first is how you win the game.
🌊

Prologue — The Mother & the Sick Father

The story opens with the younger brother, Naiee, standing at his mother's tombstone. She drowned at sea in a storm while little Naiee, in the boat, was unable to save her — which is why he fears water for the rest of the tale. His elder brother Naia calls him: their father has fallen gravely ill. Together the brothers load Father onto a cart and take him to the village doctor — who tells them the only cure is the Water of Life from the great Tree of Life.

🏘️

Chapter 1 — The Village

The journey begins through their own village and the hills beyond: a local bully, a farmer's aggressive dog, a wishing well, a caged bird and a bridge lowered by a great wheel. Working together — the elder boosting the younger up, the younger dropping ropes down — the brothers learn the game's one true rule: neither brother can do this alone.

😢

Chapter 2 — The Cave of the Trolls

In the mountains they meet a friendly troll, weeping: his wife has been captured by evil trolls. Inside the underground cave, Naiee steals the cage key from an unaware guard, frees the troll's wife, and the brothers trick the guard into the very cage he was guarding. Escaping, they trap the pursuing troll in a fiery pit. The reunited troll couple thank them and point the way onward.

🐺

Chapters 3–4 — The Woods & the Mountains

Through a dark wood of wolves and a cemetery, the brothers save a man who was about to end his own life. In the mountains they aid an inventor and fly on his glider, and they free a badly wounded griffin — which carries them onward before succumbing to its injuries.

⚔️

Chapter 5 — The Land of the Giants

The brothers cross a valley filled with the bodies of giants who died in a great war. There they rescue a young girl whom tribesmen were about to sacrifice. The girl travels with them across icy water haunted by deadly whale-like fish, repaying their kindness… or so it seems.

❄️

Chapter 6 — The Ice Lands & the Spider

In a town where a whole battle froze to death in an instant, an invisible giant stalks the brothers through the ruins. Then the girl begins to charm Naia — while Naiee, who never trusted her, watches helplessly. She lures them into a cave and reveals her true form: a monstrous spider. The brothers kill her by pulling off her legs — but not before she mortally wounds Naia, stabbing him in the stomach.

🌳

Epilogue — Sorrow: The Tree of Life

At last, the brothers reach the Tree of Life. The dying Naia insists that Naiee climb to the top alone. Naiee collects the Water of Life — but when he returns, Naia has already died. The Water cannot revive him. Naiee buries his brother beside the Tree and travels home alone. At the shoreline he must face his deepest fear: he has to swim. His mother's spirit comforts him and, guided in spirit by Naia, he forces himself across the water, gives the Water to the doctor, and his father recovers overnight. The tale ends with Naiee and his father mourning together at two tombstones — their mother's, and Naia's.

📜 Quiz Rules — How Scoring Works

Exactly how the game counts — shown live on screen after every question.

3️⃣0️⃣

30 Questions, Chapter-Wise

6 story chapters, 5 questions each. Before every chapter you see a short recap of what happened in that part of the real story — the questions are about those very events.

🔀

Different Every Time

The bank holds 42 questions (7 per chapter). Each game randomly draws 5 per chapter, shuffles their order, and shuffles the 4 answer options — so no two games are the same.

📊

Live % After Every Solve

After each question the screen updates: questions completed %, your correct-answer Score %, correct count and points — you always know exactly where you stand.

❌

Wrong Answer? Skip Ahead

A wrong answer earns 0 points and shows you the right answer. You can then skip straight to the next question. You can also skip any question before answering — skipped also counts as 0.

⭐

Only Correct Answers Count

Points come only from correct answers: +10 each. Final score is out of 300 points, with your correct count out of 30 and your final percentage.

🏅

Grades & Chapter Breakdown

At the end you get a title grade (from “Novice Traveller” to “Legend of the Valley”), a chapter-by-chapter % breakdown, and your best score is saved in the browser.

❓ FAQ

Questions players and examiners usually ask.

Are the questions really from the story?

Yes — every question is about something that happens in the real game: what the brothers saw, did, or what happened to them, chapter by chapter. Nothing is invented.

Why are the questions different when I replay?

Each chapter has a bank of 7 questions and the game randomly draws 5, in random order, with the answer options shuffled. Replaying draws a fresh set.

What happens if I answer wrong?

You get 0 points for that question, the correct answer is shown to you, and you skip ahead to the next question. Only correct answers are counted.

How is the final point calculated?

Correct answers × 10 = points, out of 300. The result screen also shows correct / 30, wrong, skipped, your percentage, a grade, and your % for each chapter.

Is the ending of the story really like this?

Yes. In the real game Naia dies at the Tree of Life, Naiee buries him, swims home alone and saves their father. There is one single, fixed ending — which is why this project's game is a quiz about the story, not a branching remake of it.
